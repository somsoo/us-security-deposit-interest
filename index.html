2

Interest earned must typically be paid out annually to the tenant or credited toward future rent.

Strict Penalties

Frequently Asked Questions

Which states require landlords to pay interest on security deposits?

States and cities including Massachusetts, New York, New Jersey, Illinois (Chicago), Connecticut, Maryland, and Washington D.C. require landlords to pay interest on held deposits.

Can a landlord deduct an administrative fee from the interest earned?

In jurisdictions like New York, landlords of buildings with 6 or more units may retain 1 percent of the deposit per year as an administrative fee, paying the balance to the tenant.

What are the penalties if a landlord fails to pay deposit interest?

In Massachusetts and Chicago, failure to pay or properly escrow security deposit interest can result in penalties up to three times the deposit amount plus attorney fees.
